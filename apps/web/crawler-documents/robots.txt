# The app host's crawler/agent policy. The site is private EXCEPT its public
# surface: the auth and legal pages below, and — since 0.4.0 — every post
# permalink (/post/<id>) and the media those pages render, which the page and
# media gates serve to signed-out fetchers too. Everything else still 302s a
# signed-out crawler to /login before any HTML ships (see SIGNED_OUT_PATHS
# and isSignedOutPath in packages/api/src/constants.ts), so there is nothing
# to disallow — privacy is enforced by the gates, not by robots.
#
# AI crawlers are allowed on the same terms as search engines: they see
# exactly the public pages and nothing else. The landing site's sitemap is
# advertised here too: the Sitemap directive may point across hosts.
User-agent: *
Allow: /

Sitemap: https://mytuums.com/sitemap.xml
Sitemap: https://about.mytuums.com/sitemap.xml
