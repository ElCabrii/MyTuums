## Talk together in groups

MyTuums 0.6.2 adds private group conversations for up to ten people.

- Create a named group from Messages and invite people to join.
- Join to see the full conversation history, including when returning after leaving.
- Everyone can invite people, rename the group, or remove a member.
- Share text, photos, voice notes, and videos with the group.
- If a blocked member is present, joining asks for confirmation. Direct-message blocks stay in place.
- Leaving or being removed closes access until you accept another invitation.
