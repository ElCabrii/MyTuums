## Welcome to Beta!

MyTuums 0.5.0 is here. Thank you for being part of the community and helping shape what comes next.

## Find your next conversation

- Explore Discover with a feed tailored to you, or switch to the latest posts. Filter by game to find your people.
- Browse popular games and upcoming releases, explore game pages, and save your favorites.

## Share more of your gaming life

- Upload videos alongside your posts, preview them before publishing, and watch them right in your feed.
- Share a post with a link, and explore quotes and reposts from profiles.
- Personalize your profile with favorite games and earned badges, with profile customization now separate from account settings.

## Make yourself at home

- Set your account to private or choose who can see each post.
- Clear individual notifications or your whole notification list.
- Enjoy smoother mobile navigation, clearer loading states, and better light-mode contrast.

See you in the feed!
