## Private conversations are here

MyTuums 0.6.0 brings direct messages, new ways to share, and optional browser notifications.

## Message your people

- Start a one-to-one conversation from someone's profile or open Messages from the navigation.
- Messages from people you don't follow arrive in Message requests. Accept to join the conversation, or decline to hide it from your inbox.
- See new messages and unread badges update live across your open tabs.
- Send images, videos, and voice messages alongside your text.
- Hide conversations, delete your own messages, and report a selected message when you need help.

## Keep your conversations across devices

Sign in on any browser or device to find your conversations and continue messaging.

## Stay in the loop

- Opt in to browser notifications from the Notifications page on supported browsers. Alerts open your notification inbox and do not include private content.
- On iPhone or iPad, add MyTuums to your Home Screen and open it there before enabling notifications.
- Browser alerts cover your notification inbox, not private messages. Enabling alerts is tied to your current session in this browser; enable them again after signing in again.

## A smoother mobile experience

Enjoy clearer messaging layouts, a composer that stays usable with the mobile keyboard, and improved image, video, and voice-message controls.

## Fixes

- Saved posts now appear reliably in Bookmarks, even when you open the page while a save is still in progress.

See you in the feed, and in your conversations!
